Famous Indian Foods

Indian cuisine is known for its rich flavours, colourful spices, and variety of dishes.
Every region of India has its own unique food traditions,
such as butter chicken and naan from North India, dosa and idli from South India, dhokla
from Gujarat, and fish curry from the coastal regions.
Popular spices like turmeric, cumin, cardamom, and coriander add distinctive flavours to Indian dishes.
Indian cuisine offers a delicious combination of taste, aroma, and cultural diversity.

Butter Chicken
A popular North Indian dish made with tender chicken cooked in a rich, creamy tomato-based gravy with aromatic spices.
Masala Dosa
A South Indian dish made from a thin and crispy fermented rice-and-lentil batter, usually filled with spicy mashed potatoes.
Biryani
A fragrant rice dish prepared with basmati rice, aromatic spices, and vegetables or meat. Different regions of India have their own styles of biryani.
